EV Taxi Fleet Transition — Sochi, Krasnodar Krai

Cost reduction, revenue protection and risk management · Analytical decision-support map · DOMA / GeoAnalyst, April 2026

Prepared by Zhanna Marchenko · NOPRIZ Lvl.7
Decision-relevant conclusions, derived from the full case study analysis.
  • Converting 60% of the fleet (120 of 200 vehicles) to Evolute i-JET EVs under a dedicated “ECO COMFORT+” tariff is projected to deliver 8–12% total fleet savings over 10 years, given night charging at RUB 3.5/kWh, 5–10 own DC fast chargers, and strict route zoning.
  • Break-even occurs at year 6–7 per vehicle; from year 6 onward each EV shifts from cost parity to net surplus versus its ICE equivalent.
  • Mountain terrain (Krasnaya Polyana) is the main operational risk: battery range drops to 180–220 km on climbs. Mitigate with a 2-trips/day cap and a dedicated local charger — do not rely on the public grid for this route.
  • Sourcing a Russian-assembled brand (Evolute, Lipetsk) removes Chinese parts-logistics risk and qualifies the fleet for preferential leasing and transport-tax exemption.
  • Krasnodar Krai's charging infrastructure is growing faster than the Russian average (+110% YoY vs national ~60–70%), reducing long-term charging-access risk for a Sochi-based fleet.
  • Recommended fleet mix: 120 EV (city/resort) + 50 hybrid (mid-range) + 30 ICE (long-distance/mountain-remote) — balances savings against operational risk rather than converting the full fleet.
